Getting started

What do I need? A NAS (a network storage device) or a computer with a shared folder (SMB), on the same network as the headset. You also need 3D movie files that you have the right to play. CineWise comes with no movies.

Which headsets? Meta Quest 3.

Who is it for? Ages 13 and up.

How do I connect? Open NAS settings. Type the address of the NAS (for example 192.168.1.20), the name of the shared folder, and the user name and password of an account on the NAS. The folder in the share is optional. Then select Connect.

Which files does it list? Files that end in .mkv, .mk3d, .mp4 or .m4v, in the folder and two levels of folders below it.

When something does not work

"The NAS did not answer." Make sure that the headset and the NAS are on the same network. Make sure that the NAS shares the folder with SMB 2 or SMB 3. Some NAS models switch SMB off by default.

"The NAS refused the user name or password." Type them again. Some NAS models need the user name in the form name and not NAS\name.

The 3D looks doubled or squashed. Open the controls and select the 3D button until the picture looks right. The layouts are Auto, H-SBS (half side-by-side), F-SBS (full side-by-side), H-TB (half top-bottom) and F-TB (full top-bottom).

Is the 3D working? Select 3D check on the home panel. Close one eye, then the other. The left eye sees red and the right eye sees blue.

There is no sound. Select the sound track button in the controls to pick another track.

A large movie does not save. The headset needs as much free space as the movie file. CineWise says how much space it needs.

Comfort and safety

  • Read Meta's health and safety warnings before you use the headset: https://www.meta.com/legal/quest/health-and-safety-warnings/
  • Watch seated or standing still, and keep the area around you clear.
  • Take regular breaks. Stop at once if you feel sick, dizzy or uncomfortable.
  • If the screen is not in front of you, hold the Meta button on the right controller to recentre the view.

Privacy

CineWise keeps your NAS sign-in details on the headset in encrypted storage, and it does not send them to us. The privacy policy lists the data that the app collects, how the app uses it and how to ask us to delete it.
